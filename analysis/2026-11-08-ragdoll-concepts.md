# Ragdoll concepts that aren't Throw Food reskins: 2026-11-08

Why ragdoll: on the radar it's 🟢 Jump in now (41 games; 25 of 40 copies reached 1K+, and 8 of the 13 newest). Ragdoll Physics went from 44.5K players this morning to 61.5K at midday. The 41 games are all sandboxes, fighting games or gory "realism" games. Almost none uses the lineage's collect → earn → upgrade loop (one 1.3K game has money and rarity), and most are bloody (🩸), which leaves a slapstick, kid-friendly lane open. Searches for every concept below returned only generic ragdoll sandboxes.

Lesson from the earlier pick: "Fling yourself for pets" swapped one part of Throw Food For Animals' loop, so it read as a reskin. The biggest hits changed theme, prize and core action at once. Each concept below changes several of those.

Checklist (0–2 each): parts from games peaking now · title in 4 words or fewer · a 5-second clip shows what's new · first reward within 30s · keeps the money/rarity/show-off loop · reason to come back tomorrow · players affect each other · buildable in 1–2 weeks.

## 1. Build a Ragdoll: 14/16 (top pick)
Open part crates, build a floppy ragdoll from rare parts, send it crashing down a giant course. Bigger crash = more cash; cash buys better crates and a stronger launcher.
- Get stronger: launcher power and course upgrades.
- Every run: the crash (the physics show).
- Prize: part crates (head / body / arms / legs) with rarities and mutations (gold, neon, jelly, giant), revealed on opening.
- Earn: parts carry cash multipliers, and finished ragdolls stand on pedestals in your base earning idle cash, offline too.
- Players: crash into others' ragdolls on the shared course for "bonk" bonuses. Fighting is more common among the ragdoll wave's winners.
- 5-second clip: a giant-headed, noodle-armed gold ragdoll bouncing down a mountain of stairs while cash pops.
- Not a reskin: a new prize (parts, not animals), you create the thing you show off, the reveal is opening crates, and the physics moment happens every run.
- Risks: assembled-part physics must look funny, not janky; the build step must be one tap (auto-equip best) so the first minute stays fast; scoring must feel fair.

## 2. Steal a Ragdoll: 12/16
Sneak into the dummy factory, grab a rare ragdoll and carry it home. It flops everywhere, and other players can slap it out of your arms. Displayed ragdolls earn cash; rarer ones earn more.
- Steal An Egg's loop, with the prize and the conflict rebuilt around physics.
- 5-second clip: two players tug-of-war over a floppy gold ragdoll; it flies off and a third player grabs it.
- Risks: sits next to the crowded steal/egg space (most copies fail, and Roblox shows lookalikes less); the thumbnail must look clearly different.

## 3. Ragdoll Hospital: 11/16
Floppy ragdoll patients come flying in from crazy accidents. Catch them on a stretcher, patch them up, get paid, grow the hospital. At night, some patients don't stay still.
- Combines three rising parts: ragdoll; shop/business tycoons (Sell Fries 42K, My Small Business 20K); horror/anomaly (36% of young games reach 1K).
- 5-second clip: a ragdoll cartwheels through the window and you dive to catch it.
- Risks: the most systems to build, the hardest to sell in one thumbnail, and it may skew toward the older horror crowd.

Dropped: ragdoll stunt park (overlaps #1 with less collecting); co-op "carry the ragdoll" obby (not in the lineage).

## Testing #1 cheaply
1. Day 1: title + icon + 2–3 thumbnails (Roblox rotates them and keeps the winner).
2. Days 2–6: minimal build. One course, 4 part slots, 3 rarities, one crate type, a launcher upgrade, display pedestals with idle cash. Starter crate on join, first crash within 15 seconds.
3. Launch with a small ad buy. Watch the first 3 days (play-through, first-play bounce, next-day return) and compare with the ragdoll copies on the radar.
4. Window: the ragdoll leader is 27 days old and Jump-in windows last weeks, so aim to ship in 7–10 days. If the radar flips ragdoll to Crowded first, rethink.

Guess for the prediction log (uncalibrated): 10K+ players within 14 days of launch ≈ 20–25%; 100K+ ≈ 3%. Base rate for young sim games that get noticed: about 5% reach 10K.
