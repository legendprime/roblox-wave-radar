## Steps 3–5: the shortlist

Checklist (0–2 points each): parts from games peaking now · title explains it in 4 words or fewer · a 5-second clip shows what's new · first reward within 30s · keeps the get-richer, rarer-pets, show-off loop · reason to come back tomorrow · players affect each other · buildable in 1–2 weeks.

### 1. Fling yourself for pets: 13/16 (top pick)
Throw Food For Animals' loop, with one slot swapped: instead of throwing food, you launch yourself as a ragdoll.
Train launch power (works while AFK) → get in the launcher and fling yourself → tumble and crash as a ragdoll → the zone you land in sets the rarity (farther means rarer) → you grab the pet you land on, and it goes to your base → pets earn money, even offline → upgrade the launcher → fling farther.
- Why it ranks first: it swaps exactly one slot of the strongest young lineage loop with the strongest uncrowded part. The ragdoll moment *is* the main action and happens every loop, not just as a side feature. Distance deciding rarity has worked twice already (Kick a Lucky Block, Throw Food).
- Title ideas: "Fling Yourself for Pets" · "Ragdoll Fling for Animals". "Ragdoll" is the word people are searching for right now.
- Risks: almost every Throw Food copy failed, so the difference has to show in the thumbnail. Ragdoll players may be older than the lineage audience. Multiplayer ragdoll physics can be fiddly. Keep it floppy and funny, with no blood (many ragdoll games use 🩸).

### 2. Ragdoll stealing: 13/16, but in a crowded space
Steal An Egg's loop, with the conflict slot swapped: when a guardian or another player hits you, you go ragdoll and the egg rolls out of your hands, and anyone can grab it.
- Great clip moment, and stealing becomes physical.
- Ranked lower because it lives in the crowded, fading steal/egg space (109 + 84 young games), the ragdoll moment only happens when you're caught, a good title is harder, and Roblox shows lookalikes less.

### 3. Steal a Ragdoll: 13/16, but at risk of looking like a reskin
Collect floppy ragdoll creatures, carry them home while they flop around, and fling and mutate them in your base (borrowing Ragdoll Research's mutations and journal).
- Clear title, and it uses the "Steal a ___" pattern.
- Ranked third because the ragdoll part is mostly the prize's look, so it risks reading as one more "Steal a ___" with a new noun (RoWatcher counted 105 of those, and the original held 92% of their players).

### Considered and dropped
Evolve-to-steal (tried, small) · Launch an egg (tried, small) · Sweep for eggs ("for eggs" titles are crowded) · Survive Your Ideas-style (too hard to build).
