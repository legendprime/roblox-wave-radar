# Grow Food For Animals: what to change so it can break out (2026-11-09)

Sources: the radar's check at 06:26 UTC today (5,526 games), the game's design doc and ledger, and the lineage notes from the ChatGPT chat.
"Max seen" means the highest player count the radar recorded on Nov 8. All-time peaks come from the lineage doc.

## Short answer
The structure is right: take the theme that's peaking (animals) and a loop from an earlier hit (Plants & Brainrots). That's exactly how Throw Food For Animals was made.
But as designed today, it's the version of that idea that has already been tried twice and flopped. Its title and its main moment also read as Throw Food For Animals.
It needs one visible twist that neither game has, a new name built around that twist, and a launch soon.

## What the evidence says

### Plants & Brainrots *has* had its animal version, twice
| Game | Created | Loop (from its description) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch Food For Animals | 2025-09-28 | Grow food in your garden, shoot it at animals to collect them, animals earn cash | 57K visits in total; 0 playing |
| Bait an Animal | 2026-09-13 | Grow bait, launch it with a harpoon, collect animals; zones, sizes, mutations | 77K visits; 48 playing |
| Feed to Steal | 2026-09-24 | Throw bait, tame pets, bonk players and steal their pets | 6.3K max seen |

Both "garden feeds animals" games were probably low-effort, and yours looks far better. Still, they show the combination by itself isn't what pulls players in.

### Why Throw Food For Animals worked where these didn't
- **Timing:** it was created on Sep 18, the week of Steal An Egg's all-time record (14.3M on Sep 19). It took the hottest theme at its peak.
- **Active action:** the player throws, and training makes them throw farther. Throw distance is the progress, and you can see it.
- **The clip:** food arcing far across a field, then an animal running over. Easy to read in 5 seconds.
- **Every step was already familiar:** Kick a Lucky Block's distance-for-rarity loop, plus Steal An Egg's training (pull-up stands instead of a treadmill).

### Where the theme stands now
- Steal An Egg: 1.26M max seen, about 9% of its peak.
- Throw Food For Animals: 374K max seen, about 24% of its ~1.56M peak.
- Plants & Brainrots: 5.1K max seen; its peak was 8.65M. Plants & Brainrots-style games aren't rising.
- The radar's "animals" wave is **Crowded**: 56 games, and only 10 of 54 copies reached 1K players. The "throw" wave: 2 of 41.
- The animal theme still has the most players on Roblox. Plain "___ for Animals" copies mostly fail now, but a clearly new angle can still get noticed.

### What's rising that fits your game
- **Eat or care to grow bigger:** Bug Evolution (36.9K max seen, 31 days old: "eat food to grow bigger"), Raise The Biggest Family (28.4K, 27 days: "your children age offline, come back daily").
- **Giant sizes as the prize:** Durian Garden (15K, 19 days: "grow GIANT durians"). Size was also central to Grow a Garden and to Steal An Egg's pets.
- **Night danger in a day job:** Sell Fries [Anomaly] (43K, 49 days: "be careful when night falls"), Animal Hospital (Anomaly) (26K). Durian Garden adds a night "purge" where players steal crops.
- **The farm branch is warming up:** the "farm" wave is Watching (3 of 9 copies hit 1K). Run A Farm hit 18.6K, and Farm Evolution is rising (6.6K at 23 days old).

## Your game against the family's stages
| Stage | Grow Food For Animals today | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Get stronger | Buy plants and rows | Fine. Plants & Brainrots had no training stat either. |
| Get the prize | Plants auto-feed animals in your lane | Passive, and it looks like Throw Food (food flying at animals). |
| Prize | Animals with rarity, mutation and a random size | Fine, but nothing you can see sets it apart. |
| Earn | Pen income, offline too | Fine. |
| Players affect each other | Nothing ("you can't lose anything") | Every 14M+ game in the line had it. |
| Title | "Grow Food For Animals" | One word away from Throw Food For Animals. Roblox shows lookalikes less, and players will read it as a copy. |
| The twist | Cravings (the right plant tames faster) | A rule, not a sight. It doesn't show in a thumbnail or a 5-second clip. |

## What to change (most important first)

### 1. Make size the prize: feed them HUGE (must)
Your design already says animals "visibly get rounder as they eat". Make that the whole hook.
- Keep Hunger for taming: fill it, get "FULL!", and the animal is tamed, as now.
- After it's full, the animal keeps walking toward the pen gate, and every extra bite grows it a step: Normal → Big → Huge → Giant → Colossal. Placeholder income: ×1, ×2, ×4, ×8, ×20.
- It joins your pen at the size it reached. That replaces the random Big/Giant spawn roll. Mutations stay random.
- Craved food grows it fastest, so cravings get a payoff you can see.
- The Snack Stick pushes a rare over the next size line, giving you an active part in the best moment.
- The Snack Bomb (animals stop and eat) now buys growth time. It already exists.
- One simple choice: tap an animal to focus every plant on it. That means growing this one huge or taming the next one.
- Tutorial: the first rabbit goes past FULL and grows to Big, so the twist lands in minute one.

Why: it takes the "eat to grow bigger" stage that's rising right now, and size as value, which every top game used. Your garden decides the size, and the result is visible from across the map.
**5-second clip:** a cow waddling down a row of plants, ballooning bigger with every bite until it towers over the player. "COLOSSAL!"

### 2. A new name and thumbnail built on that moment (must)
Drop "Food For Animals". Options, 2–4 words each: **Feed Animals HUGE**, **Grow Giant Animals**, **Chonky Animals**.
Avoid "fat" (Get FAT for Animals! and Fat for Eggs exist and stayed small, and it's touchier for kids). "Grow A Giant Pet" exists too, though it's tiny.
The thumbnail is a tiny player next to a colossal round animal, with food flying in. Upload 2–3 versions and let Roblox keep the winner.

### 3. Rare animals go to the best garden (should)
When a Legendary or better spawns, announce it to the whole server with its craving: "A Mythic Panda is coming. It craves Bamboo!"
It walks to the plot with the most or biggest of that plant, and everyone sees it arrive.
Players rush the seed shop, Grow a Garden's best social moment, and your garden becomes something you compete with. It keeps your rule that nobody loses what they own.

### 4. Fewer things in the first ten minutes (must)
Steal An Egg is about five ideas. You have seeds, restock, rows, cravings, Hunger, hotbar placing, the pen, a gear shop with three items, the Snack Stick, zones, bosses, rebirth, Index, mutations and sizes.
- Keep them, but keep the gear shop, bosses and rebirth out of sight until about 20–30 minutes in.
- Add nothing beyond items 1 and 3. If you add stealing (item 6), take something else out.

### 5. Ship within about two weeks (must)
The game is mostly built, so items 1–3 are days of work, not weeks.
The theme's window is closing: the newest animal copies mostly fail, and Steal An Egg is fading.

### 6. Your call: stealing at night
Stealing has the strongest record in the family: Steal a Brainrot 25.8M, Steal An Egg 14.3M, Don't Wake the Brainrots 522K. "When night falls" games are rising right now.
- One version: at night, pen animals sleep, and you can sneak into another pen and lure one out with its craved food. If it wakes, it runs home.
- The owner's pen can lock for 60 seconds, the way Steal a Brainrot's bases do.

This breaks your design's "you cannot lose anything you own", and young players may quit after losing a giant. I'd leave it out of the launch and test it as the first big update, if the launch numbers are decent.

## Keep as is
Plants & Brainrots' structure (money buys plants, plants get animals, animals earn money), the seed-shop restock timer, cravings, mutations, pen income and offline pay, the Index, zones, and the sculpted animals.
The art quality is a real edge: the flops above look cheap.

## Don't add
A treadmill or training stat (plants are your "get stronger"), eggs, or more currencies.

## Odds (uncalibrated guesses)
- About 5% of noticed young simulation games reach 10K players. That's the base rate.
- **As designed today:** about 5% to reach 10K within 14 days of launch. The same concept already flopped, the theme is crowded and the title is a lookalike. The art quality pulls the other way.
- **With changes 1–5:** about 10–15% for 10K, and about 1–2% for 100K+.
- These are guesses. The first 72 hours of real players will tell us far more than any analysis.

## Testing it
1. Thumbnails first: 2–3 versions of the giant-animal shot.
2. Build items 1, 2 and 4 first, and check in play that the growing looks great on the sculpted meshes, including on a phone.
3. Launch with a small ad buy. Watch play-through, first-play bounce, next-day return and session length.
4. Compare with the animals wave's copies on the radar. If it beats most of them in week one, add item 3, then try item 6.
