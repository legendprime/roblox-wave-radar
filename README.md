## How to read this

- A **wave** is a group of games made in the last 60 days whose titles share an idea (ragdoll, steal, for animals...).
- **Copies 1K+** counts how many of the wave's newer games (3+ days old) reached 1,000 players. If most copies succeed, players want more of this; if most fail, it's crowded.
- **🟢 Jump in now:** at least 2 copies reached 1K+, at least 40% of copies succeed, the leader is under 60 days old, and players aren't falling.
- **🌱 Just starting:** leader under 30 days old with at most 8 games so far.
- **🔴 Crowded:** 80+ games, or under 25% of copies succeeding, or the leader is over 75 days old.
- **⚫ Fading:** total players down more than 25% in a week.
- The radar only sees games that appear in Roblox's charts or search. It adds each hot wave's words to its next searches so the small copies get counted too.
- None of this guarantees a hit: it tells you **when** a wave is open. The idea and the twist are still yours.
